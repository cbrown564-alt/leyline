# Leyline site plan

Agreed 11 October 2026. Leyline is a software studio, and nothing else.

## Remove

- Local growth (page and homepage band). Revisit only if Killian wants it back.
- Fluff: taglines, the "era of bespoke" paragraph, unsupported stats, "human, always", the "Every engagement" grid, the FAQ, the service-picker cart.
- The 13 "peace of mind" promises in their current form: most are sensible suggestions rather than how the studio actually works.

## Five blocks

1. **What we do.** Custom software for a business, in one or two lines.
2. **Work.** Case studies built on screenshots and outcomes. First: ENI (`case-studies/eni.md`). Live-events work to follow, once screenshots and outcomes exist.
3. **How we work.** One horizontal strip, standard process, very little text.
4. **People.** Killian (London) and Conor (Belfast): photo, name, a line each. Placeholders until supplied; this is essential, not optional.
5. **Contact.** Short form plus email.

Also:
- **Project sizes:** three example sizes (small, medium, large), each with a typical length and price range. Placeholders until agreed.
- **Terms:** a small visual summary of only what is genuinely true of how we work. To be agreed with Killian.
- **Privacy:** separate page, plumbing.
